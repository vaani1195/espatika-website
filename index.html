What we do

Websites

Fast websites that look right on any phone, show up on Google, and cost very little to host. Good for a business that needs to look credible online and be easy to contact.

  • Design and build
  • Phone and tablet layout
  • Domain and hosting set up for you
  • Basic search engine setup

SEO

Get your website properly set up for Google to find, understand, and index. We audit what’s holding it back, fix the technical and on-page SEO, and show you what changed with clear before-and-after reporting.

  • Google Search Console setup
  • Sitemap setup and submission
  • Keyword and on-page SEO optimisation
  • Structured data setup
  • Indexing and technical SEO fixes
  • Before-and-after SEO audit report

AI tools

Custom tools built on language models, shaped around one job in your business. For example, an assistant that answers questions from your own documents, or a tool that drafts routine replies for your team to check and send.

  • A working prototype first, so you can try it early
  • Live hosting once you are happy
  • Notes on how it works and what it costs to run

Data analysis

We take your sales or operations data and answer one clear business question. What will demand look like next quarter? Which customers are likely to leave? What is really driving your results?

  • We agree the question with you before touching the data
  • Data cleaning, forecasting and prediction models
  • Findings explained in plain language, with a written handover
